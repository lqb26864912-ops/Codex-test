Financial requirements: the deposit or income requirements for a retirement visa must be met with the applicant’s own genuine funds or income, according to current immigration rules. False documents are never a substitute. We explain the requirements and help prepare documents; immigration makes the decision.

Processing routes

Two routes to choose from

01Same-day

if eligible

Express route

If you meet the requirements, the application can be submitted on the same day. Approvals of up to 15 months are common.

023–5 days

typical timeframe

Standard route

You attend in person about twice; it usually takes 3–5 days.

Which route applies depends on your entry status, remaining stay and documents, and is confirmed case by case.

i

Visa validity ≠ permitted stay

The validity of a visa, the length of stay granted on entry, later extensions and re-entry requirements should each be checked separately.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Eligibility, fees and scope of service are confirmed case by case.

My financial requirements are not in place yet. Can I still ask?＋

Yes. We help you understand immigration’s financial requirements and prepare compliant financial documents. The requirements must be met with your own genuine funds or income.

What is the difference between the express and standard routes?＋

On the express route, if you are eligible, the application can be submitted on the same day; approvals of up to 15 months are common, but immigration decides. The standard route requires attending about twice and usually takes 3–5 days. Which route applies is confirmed case by case.

Can you guarantee approval or entry?＋

No. Visas, extensions, work permits and entry are decided by the relevant authorities.

How are your fees calculated?＋

We quote case by case, based on your situation and the scope of service. Official fees, our service fee and any other costs are listed separately and confirmed before we start.

Are you an embassy, the immigration office or an official agent?＋

No. We provide consultation, document preparation and application assistance. We are not part of any government, embassy or immigration office, and we do not claim any official affiliation, authorisation or partnership with visa-issuing authorities.

Do I need to send documents for the first consultation?＋

No. A rough description of your situation, your current city and your timing is enough. Please do not enter passport numbers or bank details in the form, and do not upload ID documents.
