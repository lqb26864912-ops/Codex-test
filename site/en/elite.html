Who it suits

The Elite visa suits people who…

01

Plan to live here long term

Depending on the package, validity runs from 5 to 20+ years, which suits people who see Thailand as a long-term base.

02

Travel to Thailand often

Long-term, multiple-entry: for people who come and go often for family, business or lifestyle.

03

Value member services

Airport VIP, the Elite Personal Liaison (EPL), multilingual support and more (usage and scope depend on the package).

Official application steps

Four steps to apply

  1. 01

    Apply

    Passport copy (at least 1 year of validity), photo with white or blue background, application form.

  2. 02

    Review

    Reviewed by the Department of Consular Affairs; officially, approval is issued within about 2 months.

  3. 03

    Pay & activate

    Pay after approval; membership is activated within about 1 week.

  4. 04

    Visa affixation

    On arrival at Bangkok, Phuket or Chiang Mai international airports, at Thai embassies/consulates, or at Chaeng Watthana Immigration in Bangkok.

Applications are reviewed by Thailand Privilege Card Co., Ltd. and the Thai authorities.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Eligibility, fees and scope of service are confirmed case by case.

How is the Elite visa different from the retirement visa and DTV?＋

They suit different situations: the Elite visa is a membership long-stay visa, the retirement visa is for life in retirement, and DTV is for remote work or certain activities. See the comparison above. If you are unsure, try the visa matcher or send us your situation.

How long does the application take?＋

Officially, approval is issued within about 2 months after review, and membership is activated within about 1 week of payment. Actual timing follows the official process.

Can I apply together with my family?＋

Platinum, Diamond and Reserve include supplementary memberships; see the fees in the package table above. Whether your family members qualify, and how to apply, follows the official requirements; ask us.

How is my personal information handled?＋

A first enquiry does not require ID documents. Information you give us is used only to reply to your enquiry and handle your application; see our Privacy Notice.

Do I need a bank deposit or income proof for the Elite visa?＋

The official application documents are a passport copy, a photo and the application form; the official requirements prevail.

Can the official prices change?＋

Yes. Thailand Privilege reserves the right to change packages, benefits and conditions; always refer to the latest official announcement.

Can you guarantee approval or entry?＋

No. Visas, extensions, work permits and entry are decided by the relevant authorities.

How are your fees calculated?＋

We quote case by case, based on your situation and the scope of service. Official fees, our service fee and any other costs are listed separately and confirmed before we start.

Are you an embassy, the immigration office or an official agent?＋

We are not part of any government, embassy or immigration office, and we do not claim official affiliation, authorisation or partnership with a visa-issuing authority. We help compare packages, check documents and submit applications. Membership approval is decided by Thailand Privilege Card Co., Ltd. and the Thai authorities.

Do I need to send documents for the first consultation?＋

No. A rough description of your situation, your current city and your timing is enough. Please do not enter passport numbers or bank details in the form, and do not upload ID documents.
