Privacy Notice

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This notice explains how magnificentvisa.com handles your information. We keep collection to a minimum: our enquiry form does not send what you type to our servers.

2. What this website processes

  • Enquiry form: what you type is used only to build a draft message in your own browser. It is not sent to our servers and is cleared when you refresh the page. The draft is handed to a messaging app only when you click a button such as “Open in WhatsApp”, and you decide whether to send it.
  • Information you send us: messages you send through WhatsApp, Telegram, LINE, Messenger or WeChat reach us, and we use them to answer your enquiry, give a quote and carry out the work. For a first enquiry, please do not send passport numbers or bank details, and do not upload ID documents.
  • Page-view statistics: we use GoatCounter to count page views and similar summary figures. According to the service, it does not use cookies to track visitors.
  • Ad measurement: we use the Meta Pixel, described in the next section.
  • Server logs: like most websites, our server may keep basic logs such as access time, IP address and the page requested, for security and troubleshooting.

3. Meta Pixel (Facebook / Instagram ads)

This website uses the Meta Pixel provided by Meta Platforms. It sets cookies in your browser and sends Meta the pages you visit, browser and device information, your IP address, and events when you click a contact button (WhatsApp, Telegram, LINE, Messenger, copy WeChat ID) or use the enquiry form. These events contain only the contact channel, the service page you were on and the page language; we have not set the Pixel to send the text you type into the form.

This data is used to measure how our ads perform and to show relevant ads to people who have visited this website. Meta handles the data under its own privacy policy.

How to opt out: in your Facebook or Instagram ad settings you can turn off ads based on website activity. You can also block third-party cookies in your browser or use an ad blocker.

4. Who receives information

Besides the messaging apps, Meta and GoatCounter mentioned above, the website is served from a hosting server. When we carry out a specific application, we submit the documents it needs to the relevant government authority with your agreement. We do not sell your information.

5. How long we keep it

We keep chat records and application documents only for as long as needed to handle your enquiry, provide the service and meet legal requirements.

6. Your rights

Under applicable data protection law (for example Thailand’s Personal Data Protection Act, PDPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, or to withdraw your consent. Please tell us using the contact details above.

7. Changes to this notice

If the tools we use or the way we handle information changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
