Thailand DTV Visa

DTV is a Thai visa category for people who work remotely or take part in certain activities in Thailand. You apply from outside Thailand, through the Thai e-Visa system, to the embassy or consulate that covers where you live. We help you check the category, review the document list and follow the application.

What to know first

Who DTV is for, and where to apply

Typical purposes

One route is remote work or freelance work (often called Workcation), where you stay in Thailand but your employer or clients are abroad. Another covers certain activities in Thailand, such as a course, training or a medical arrangement. Which one applies depends on your situation and the document list of the post you use. Please do not assume you automatically fit a category.

Where you apply

DTV applications are filed through the Thai e-Visa system (thaievisa.go.th) with the Thai embassy or consulate that covers your place of residence. You generally cannot file this visa through the system while you are in Thailand. Choosing the wrong post, or applying from a place that does not match the residence requirement, often means starting again, so this step should be checked first.

Documents

The lists published by different posts are not identical. Typical items include the passport biodata page, a recent photo, proof of residence in the country where you apply, financial evidence, documents that match your purpose, and a criminal-record certificate. Tell us your nationality, where you live and your purpose, and we will check the list that post is currently using.

FAQ

Questions we hear first

I am already in Thailand. Can I still apply for a DTV?＋

A DTV is filed from outside Thailand through the e-Visa system to the relevant post. You generally cannot file it while you are in Thailand. Tell us your current visa and stay, and we will look at what you can do next.

How is DTV different from a retirement or Elite visa?＋

They are aimed at different situations. A retirement visa is usually tied to age and financial conditions, the Elite visa is a Thailand Privilege membership, and DTV relates to remote work or certain activities. If you are unsure, use the visa matcher or send us your situation.

Will I need a 90-day report after approval?＋

If you stay in Thailand on a DTV for a continuous period that reaches the reporting threshold, you usually need to report your address to immigration. We can help with that as well; see the 90-day report page.

How are your fees calculated?＋

We quote case by case. Official fees, our service fee and any other costs are listed separately and confirmed before we start. This page does not list prices.

Are you an embassy, the immigration office or an official agent?＋

We provide consultation, document preparation and application assistance. We are not part of any government, embassy or immigration office, and we do not claim official affiliation, authorisation or partnership with a visa-issuing authority.

Do I need to send documents for the first consultation?＋

No. A rough description of your situation, your current city and your timing is enough. Please do not enter passport numbers or bank details in the form, and do not upload ID documents.
