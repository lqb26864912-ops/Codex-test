Who needs to report, and how we help

When a report is due

Holders of long-stay visas who remain in Thailand for a continuous period must report their current address to immigration. This can apply to retirement, Elite, DTV, work and student visas, among others. Whether it is your turn depends on the visa type and the date you entered or last reported. We will check that with you.

The filing window

Reports have a fixed window: not too early, and not after the deadline. How the window is counted depends on your visa and your record. If you leave Thailand and come back, the start date may also change. You can send the visa page, your latest entry stamp and the previous report receipt for us to check. Please do not send document numbers in the first message.

What we handle

We prepare form TM47 and file the report. Whether that is in person, by post or another method, and whether you need to attend, depends on your visa type and the rules in force. We will explain this before we start. Please do not assume that someone else’s method will apply to you.

If you also need an extension or a re-entry permit, or you are looking at a DTV, retirement visa or Elite visaDTV, tell us at the same time and we will plan around your travel dates.

FAQ

Questions we hear first

Is the first report the same as later ones?＋

Not always. The first report and later reports can differ in whether you must attend, which documents are needed and how they are filed. Check against your own visa and record rather than copying someone else’s experience.

What if I miss the window?＋

Send us your visa type and the relevant dates as soon as you can, and we will look at the next step with you. Do not delay, and do not trust anyone who says an overstay can simply be cleared from inside the country.

If I leave and come back, do I still use the old date?＋

Leaving and re-entering can change when the period starts. Send us your latest arrival and departure stamps and we will recalculate it with you.

How are your fees calculated?＋

We quote case by case. Official fees, our service fee and any other costs are listed separately and confirmed before we start. This page does not list prices.

Are you an embassy, the immigration office or an official agent?＋

We provide consultation, document preparation and application assistance. We are not part of any government, embassy or immigration office, and we do not claim official affiliation, authorisation or partnership with a visa-issuing authority.

Do I need to send documents for the first consultation?＋

No. Your visa type, rough dates and current city are enough. Please do not enter passport numbers or bank details in the form, and do not upload ID documents.
