This is an enquiry page, not an open processing channel

New processing requests are paused

We are not taking on new work for medical stays or medical extensions at the moment. This page is here so you can still describe your situation. After we read it, we will tell you whether we can help just now, or what you should check with the treating hospital.

There is more than one possible route

Treatment or recovery in Thailand may involve a medical visa or a medical extension, and in some cases another visa category. Which route fits depends on the treatment plan, how long you need to stay and your current immigration status. Please do not assume that someone else’s approval will apply to you.

What to prepare for an enquiry

You do not need to send medical details in the first message. If you already have a hospital appointment or a letter, tell us the type of document and the date. Please do not upload ID documents or a full medical record at this stage. A letter from the treating hospital is usually the starting point for any later document check.

The results page includes earlier medical-extension cases, with personal data masked. Those records show work already completed; they do not mean we are taking new requests now. See client results. If you are also looking at a DTV or another type of stay, mention that too.

FAQ

Questions we hear first

Can you process a medical visa for me now?＋

We are not taking new processing requests at the moment. You can still send us your situation and the type of hospital documents you have. We will tell you whether we can help just now, or what to check with the hospital.

Why are there medical-extension cases on the results page?＋

Those are records of work already completed, with personal data masked. They show that this type of application has been handled before; they do not mean we are open to new requests now.

Can I ask before I have a hospital letter?＋

Yes. A rough description of what you need and how long you plan to stay is enough. If a hospital letter is needed, we will tell you what to ask the hospital to confirm; the hospital issues it based on your treatment.

Are you a hospital, an embassy or the immigration office?＋

No. We are a private consultancy. We are not a hospital, a government body, an embassy or the immigration office, and we do not claim official affiliation, authorisation or partnership with a visa-issuing authority. Treatment is a matter for the hospital; visa or extension decisions are made by the competent authorities.

Do I need to send documents or medical records for the first consultation?＋

No. A rough description of your situation, your current city and your timing is enough. Please do not enter passport numbers or bank details, and do not upload ID documents or a full medical record.
